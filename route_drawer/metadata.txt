about=Click Draw Route, then click points on the map. With Follow Roads on, each new click connects to the previous one along the shortest path on your road layer (auto-picked by name, e.g. gis_osm_roads_free_1; change it from the options button); Shift+click draws a straight segment. Hold the left button (or drag) to draw freehand: points follow the cursor until you release, and one right-click removes the whole stroke. Arrow keys or W/A/S/D move the map a quarter view while drawing, even mid-stroke. Right-click (or Backspace) undoes the last point, Esc cancels, press Enter to save. While drawing, a label beside the cursor shows the total length and the distance to the cursor in metres (ellipsoidal), also shown in the status bar; saved routes are labelled with their length in metres (toggle in the options menu). Saving opens a small Save window: name, remarks, colour and line width (colour and width default to your last choice); each route is drawn in its own colour and width and keeps them in KMZ export; remarks become the placemark description (balloon text), and pins export as Google Earth pushpins in their own colour and size. Routes are saved into the group selected in the Layers panel (the selected group, or the group of the selected layer; the top level otherwise): each group keeps its own Drawn routes layer and file (e.g. drawn_routes_sub-group1.gpkg), made only when you press Save, and the Save window says where the route goes. The Layers panel shows one entry per colour used, labelled with the routes in it (updated after each save or edit). If the top-level Drawn routes layer was taken off the map, the next save starts a new dated file and says where the previous one is kept; options button > Save routes in… opens a file again, in the selected group. Routes are saved with name, length_m, method, created, remarks, color and width_mm fields to a "Drawn routes" GeoPackage layer (~/Documents/QGIS Drawn Routes/drawn_routes.gpkg by default), or into the selected line layer when it is in edit mode. The Import/Export button imports a CSV as pins (latitude/longitude columns found automatically, also degrees-minutes-seconds, decimal commas and one combined column) and a KMZ/KML with all ExtendedData, folders and colours (read piece by piece, so even very large KMZ files work), and exports what is highlighted in the Layers panel (one layer, several layers, or a whole group such as pins + lines: one KMZ folder or Excel sheet per layer) to a standard KMZ (map colours, names, descriptions, all attributes, folders) or to Excel (all attributes plus coordinates, lengths or areas). Pins in boundary (toolbar button) opens a panel: choose a region, province, town/city or barangay (NAMRIA/PSA boundaries from HDX, read straight from the downloaded zip; OpenStreetMap admin areas as a fallback), a polygon of any layer, or draw your own (kept in My boundaries for next time), one or several at once; Select pins selects the pins inside in the ticked point layers (a pin on the line counts as inside), and Extract copies them to a new layer, KMZ and/or Excel, each pin with a 'boundary' column naming the boundary it is in. The Info tool shows all data of a clicked feature; links such as GDrive open in the browser. The chosen road layer is remembered between sessions by its file: next time it is found again in the project, or, if it is not loaded, its file is used in the background without adding it to the map. The import folder is remembered too. Respects the project's snapping settings. The road layer is only read. Works on QGIS 3.22+ (Qt5) and QGIS 4 (Qt6), on Windows and Mac.
